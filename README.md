# Rainfall Dashboard

A dashboard about yearly rainfall for 184 countries, grouped by continent.

## The problem
Rainfall is easy to compare when you can see it by continent and by country side by side. I wanted a simple way to click into any country and see its rainfall and rating.

## What you can explore
- Rainfall by continent (bar chart)
- Rainfall by country (bar chart)
- A country picker with each country's continent
- A star rating for rainfall per country

## What I found
The total across all countries is about 219K mm per year, and the two bar charts show how much rainfall differs from one continent or country to another.
